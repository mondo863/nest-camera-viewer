Privacy Policy

Nest Camera Viewer is a personal app used only by its owner.

  • It uses Google's Smart Device Management API only to list the owner's Nest cameras and show their live video on the owner's TV.
  • Video plays live and is never recorded or stored.
  • The Google sign-in is stored only on the owner's own PC and TV.
  • No data is collected, sold or shared with anyone.

Access can be removed at any time from your Google Account's third-party access settings, or in the Google Home app under partner connections.
